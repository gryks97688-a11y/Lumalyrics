Spotify & appearance

Connect your Spotify account to automatically display the currently playing song. Spotify provides playback metadata and controls, but not lyrics through this API. Automatic lyrics use independent LRCLIB when available; Spotify does not provide lyrics through its API. Word timing is available only when the lyric data contains word timestamps.

Redirect URI to register in your Spotify dashboard:

Create an app at developer.spotify.com/dashboard, add the exact redirect URI above, and add your Spotify account to the app's allowlist if required. No client secret is needed. Spotify API eligibility and quotas are determined by Spotify.
